Risk Desk

Browser game · prototype · November 2026

A one-screen game about position sizing. A random market trends or chops, and each round you choose how much of your account to risk, then go long, short or pass. You get 20 rounds to grow the account, and the run ends early if you fall 20% below your peak.

The idea comes from what I study: picking direction matters less than how much you bet and when you sit out.

Built with
Claude Code (AI coding agent), plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on a canvas. No game engine.
What I did
Chose the concept from my finance background and directed the build.
What AI did
Claude Code wrote the code and the first pass of the rules and numbers.
Play time
About two minutes per run. Works with mouse, touch or keyboard.
